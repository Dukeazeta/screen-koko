Built so you never alt‑tab mid‑sentence.

Screen Koko is a small AI assistant that lives in a notch at the top of your screen. It hears the call, reads your screen when you ask, and writes a short answer you can use straight away. The app you're in keeps the keyboard the whole time.

  • How it answersIt streams your mic and the call audio to Google Gemini Live and shows the reply as text in the notch.
  • ProfilesInterview, Meeting, Sales, Presentation and Negotiation, each with its own way of answering. Pick how long or short replies should be.
  • Out of sightThe notch is hidden from screenshots and screen sharing, and it never steals focus from your call.
  • What gets sent whereAudio, typed questions and the screenshots you ask for go to Google Gemini. Your saved facts and conversation history stay on your PC, and you can delete them in Settings.
  • InstallingWindows 10 or 11. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway.
